# Prompt · Anuncio TikTok de Zarpa (8–10 s, vertical 9:16, UGC)

Copia todo lo que hay entre las dos líneas y pégalo en la aplicación de vídeo. Está en inglés porque casi todas las IA de vídeo lo entienden mejor; los diálogos y los textos en pantalla están en español, para tu público de España.

---

**GOAL OF THE AD**
Create an 8–10 second vertical (9:16) UGC-style ad for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. It must stop the scroll in the first 1–2 seconds with a "wow" moment, make viewers fall in love with the product, and make them want to buy it right now as a Christmas gift. It should feel like a real person filmed it on a phone at home, not like a polished commercial.

**PRODUCT (show it exactly like this – do not change the material)**
- Name: "Zarpa" house slippers by the brand Zaldori.
- Look: closed-toe clog-style house slippers with a cute minimalist cat face (small embossed whiskers, tiny nose) and two small rounded cat ears on top. Colours: cream white or soft butter yellow.
- Outside material: smooth, glossy EVA rubber foam – lightweight, waterproof, wipe-clean. The exterior is NOT fluffy, NOT furry, NOT plush.
- Inside: a soft, warm, cream-coloured plush/fleece lining that is removable and washable (visible only at the opening of the slipper).
- Sole: 3 cm soft non-slip EVA sole shaped like a cat paw (four toe beans + one big paw pad). **Key feature: when you step on a wet floor, the sole leaves little cat paw prints behind you.**
- Other true benefits: 5 layers of warmth, waterproof, non-slip, very light.
- Sizes: EU 36–37, 38–39, 40–41. Price: €34.99. Free shipping to Spain.

**UGC CREATOR (model)**
A relatable woman, 22–30, natural and friendly, minimal make-up, hair in a messy bun, wearing cosy pyjamas or loungewear (cream or red-and-white Christmas pattern). Authentic, excited, slightly surprised energy – like she is showing her best friend something adorable she just discovered. Handheld phone camera, a little natural shake, warm natural light, cosy Spanish apartment decorated for Christmas (fairy lights, small Christmas tree in the background).

**SHOT LIST (9 seconds)**
- **0.0–2.0 s – THE WOW HOOK:** extreme close-up, camera at floor level on a wet, shiny tile floor (bathroom or kitchen, warm light). A slipper steps into frame in slow motion and lifts away, revealing a perfect little cat paw print in water on the floor. Then a second print appears… and a trail of cat paw prints leads toward the camera. Water droplets sparkle. On-screen text (big, bold, centred): **"Espera… ¿¿son huellas de gato?? 🐾"**
- **2.0–4.5 s – REVEAL:** the camera tilts up to show the creator wearing the cream cat-face slippers, wiggling her feet happily. She flips one slipper over to the camera to show the paw-shaped sole close up. Text: **"Son mis zapatillas 😭"**
- **4.5–7.0 s – PROOF OF BENEFITS (fast cuts, 0.8 s each):**
  1. Water splashes on the slipper and she wipes it clean with one swipe → text **"Impermeables por fuera"**
  2. She pulls out the soft plush lining → text **"Peluche calentito por dentro (y se lava)"**
  3. She walks confidently on the wet floor → text **"No resbalan"**
- **7.0–9.0 s – CALL TO ACTION:** she holds both slippers up next to her face near the Christmas tree, smiling at the camera. Text: **"Zarpa · 34,99 € · Envío gratis 🎄"**, then **"El regalo más tierno de esta Navidad – link en bio"**. Small "ZALDORI" logo at the bottom.

**VOICEOVER / DIALOGUE (Spanish, natural, excited, fast)**
"¿Has visto esto? ¡Dejan huellas de gatito! 🐾 Por fuera son impermeables, por dentro peluche calentito… El regalo de Navidad perfecto. ¡Pídelas ya y llegan para Navidad!"

**STYLE AND TECHNICAL**
- Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 8–10 seconds, 24–30 fps.
- Authentic UGC TikTok aesthetic: handheld, close-ups, quick jump cuts, a whip pan between scenes.
- Warm, cosy Christmas colour grade (golden lights, cream, soft red), with sparkly water reflections.
- Big, bold on-screen captions in TikTok style (white text, black outline), kept inside the safe zones (not at the very bottom or the right edge).
- Music: upbeat, cute, trendy, with a light Christmas feel (sleigh bells), with a beat drop exactly when the paw prints appear.
- Sound effects: soft "splash" on each step, a tiny "meow" when the paw print appears.

**AVOID (negative prompt)**
Fluffy or furry exterior on the slippers, plush outer fabric, fuzzy texture outside, different shoe shapes, open-toe sandals, text spelling mistakes, distorted hands or feet, extra fingers, studio or corporate look, slow intro, logos of other brands, fake price claims.
