# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme, Liquid; mobile-first storefront)

## Users

Young adult and adult shoppers in Spain, roughly 18 to 55, who mostly browse and buy on their phone. They buy for themselves or as a gift. Their scenes: after training, at the office desk, on the sofa at night, after a long day on their feet.

## Product Purpose

Zaldori (zaldori.com, support@zaldori.com) is a small direct-to-consumer brand that sells three everyday-wellbeing objects:

- **Vortix**: rechargeable electric mixing cup, 400 ml, up to 7000 rpm, 1200 mAh USB battery. Two models (Clásico, Estilizado) and several colours.
- **TermoCalm**: 30 × 60 cm velvet heating pad, 9 heat levels (approx. 30–50 °C), timer 30/60/90/120 min with auto-off, 75 W. EU, UK or US plug.
- **Pulsia**: EMS foot massage mat, 8 modes, 19 intensity levels, remote control, USB rechargeable, ultra-thin and foldable.

Success: a visitor understands the three products in seconds, finds the one for them, chooses a variant and adds it to the cart from the same page, on a phone.

## Positioning

A single house for three small rituals (mix, warm, rest) instead of three disconnected dropshipping pages. The brand is the curator: one tone, one quality bar.

## Evidence and constraints

- Product facts come only from the live Shopify product data. No invented features, materials, results, certifications, reviews, testimonials, customer counts, discounts or scarcity.
- TermoCalm and Pulsia are comfort/wellbeing devices, not medical devices: no medical or therapeutic claims beyond the product copy, and their safety warnings must stay reachable.
- Verifiable store promises (repeated in the product copy): free shipping to Spain with tracking, 14 days to withdraw, 3-year legal guarantee, secure payment. Shipping time is unconfirmed: `[PENDIENTE]`.
- AI-generated imagery is advertising, never presented as customers or testimonials.

## Brand commitments

- Official logo: Z-shaped symbol with a dot above + "ZALDORI" in spaced capitals, raspberry #B7355E.
- Typography pinned by the owner: Manrope (headlines), Inter (text and controls); Playfair Display only very occasionally.
- Palette pinned by the owner: #B7355E as the single main accent, used with restraint; warm whites, a bone/cream tone, a warm near-black for text; at most one secondary tone derived from the pink. No random gradients.

## Voice

Spanish (Spain), tú. Calm, warm, concrete, short sentences. Confident without hype.
