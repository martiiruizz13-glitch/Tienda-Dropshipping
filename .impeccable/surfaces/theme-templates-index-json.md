# Surface: Zaldori flagship (theme/templates/index.json + page.zaldori.json)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: 18–55, Spain, mostly mobile. Action: choose a variant and add to cart for Vortix, TermoCalm or Pulsia without leaving the page.

## Direction contract

seed: seed:199c1d495daba97b (degraded roll, no challengers)

THESIS: Zaldori as an exhibition in three rooms. Each product is an object on display with its museum label (cartela); the label is the buy box. Refuses the category default of a split hero plus a row of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm white #FAF7F2 and bone #EFE8DD grounds, warm near-black #1F1A17 ink, raspberry #B7355E as the single accent, wine #6E1F3A as its dark derivative. Room tints: cool sage (Vortix), warm sand (TermoCalm), powder rose (Pulsia). Manrope tight display, Inter text. Hairline rules, room numbers (Nº 01), label typography, no shadows, no card grids.

STORY: Preloader draws the Z → hero states the house → pinned gallery walks three rooms → each room opens into its own chapter (expansion / editorial asymmetry / parallax) ending at its label-buy box and FAQ → ads → quick chooser → trust → FAQ → footer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Massive Manrope headline revealed by line mask over a large-format photo framed like an exhibited piece, room index 01/02/03 as navigation, one clear CTA to the rooms.

SIGNATURE: Pinned room sequence: frame grows from rounded inset to full bleed, background tint crossfades room to room, title mask-reveals, label slides in. Reversible scrub.

RISKS: Pins on mobile; replace with full-height snap-free rooms and in-view reveals. Real photos are square supplier shots; frame them with object-fit and tint grounds.
